RESEARCH PROFILE

Environmental physiologist and quantitative researcher working at the intersection of heat exposure, sleep and population health. Currently Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore, where I provide analytical leadership across two national research programmes — Project HEATS and Cooling Singapore 2.0 — modelling how the indoor thermal environment shapes sleep and physiological strain in free-living tropical populations. My work spans large longitudinal wearable and environmental datasets, randomised cooling interventions, and evidence synthesis that feeds directly into heat-health advisory policy. I hold a PhD in Sport, Health and Exercise Sciences, publish across environmental physiology and nutritional science, supervise three doctoral candidates and a postdoctoral researcher, and lead strategic industry partnerships for the NUS Medicine Early Career Researchers Office.
