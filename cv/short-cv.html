Dr Michèle Christopher Renard

mrenard@nus.edu.sg | +65 8058 9233 | Singapore (Permanent Resident) LinkedIn | ORCID 0000-0003-4517-1316 | Google Scholar | ResearchGate

Environmental physiologist and quantitative researcher. I model how the built thermal environment affects sleep, physiological strain and health in real-world populations. At the National University of Singapore I lead the statistical analysis for two national research programmes, working across large longitudinal wearable and sensor datasets, randomised field interventions, and evidence synthesis that feeds national heat-health policy. PhD in Sport, Health and Exercise Sciences; eleven peer-reviewed publications; four PROSPERO-registered systematic reviews; three doctoral candidates and a postdoctoral researcher under supervision.

EXPERIENCE

Research Fellow — National University of Singapore, Yong Loo Lin School of Medicine Human Potential Translational Research Programme & Heat Resilience and Performance Centre | Apr 2025 – Present

  • Analytical lead across two NRF-funded national programmes — Project HEATS and Cooling Singapore 2.0 — owning the statistical workflow from analysis plan to publication.
  • Lead the flagship analysis of indoor heat exposure and sleep across 122 participants and 10,303 person-nights (three field waves, Jun 2025 – Jul 2026), using mixed-effects models with Mundlak decomposition to separate behavioural adaptation from physiological strain, and spline and segmented methods to locate non-linear thresholds.
  • Model laboratory heat-exposure responses across vulnerable population groups (children, unacclimatised adults, older adults) and translate outputs into policy recommendations shaping Singapore’s national heat-health advisories.
  • Lead four systematic reviews and supervise four early-career researchers; work featured in The Straits Times and Lianhe Zaobao following the NRF CREATE Symposium 2026.

Postdoctoral Research Associate — King’s College London, Department of Nutritional Sciences Jul 2024 – Apr 2025 (Research Assistant, Feb – Jul 2024)

  • Led research on cooking and food behaviours during the UK cost-of-living crisis, from recruitment and data collection through to publication.
  • Delivered mixed-methods analysis across large survey datasets — hierarchical regression, non-parametric group comparison, and thematic analysis of focus group and open-text data.
  • Registered and led a scoping review on food and sustainability living laboratories in higher education.

Assistant Lecturer — Atlantic Technological University, Sligo, Ireland | Oct 2021 – Jun 2022 Module lead for MSc Advanced Performance Nutrition; lectured across undergraduate exercise physiology, anatomy and nutrition; supervised ten undergraduate research projects.

Health Advisor — Bupa, UK | Mar 2018 – Jul 2019 Delivered clinical health assessments: 12-lead ECG, spirometry, sub-maximal CPET, point-of-care blood analysis (trained phlebotomist), and motivational-interviewing-based lifestyle coaching.
